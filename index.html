The Feature · ORM & AEO

Scam allegations to ‘potentially worthwhile’: how 60 videos moved Google’s AI answers.

The problem

Google’s AI Overviews showed scam allegations and mixed verdicts before people reached the website.

The insight

AI answers summarize what the web says. So I changed the sources, not the answer.

What I did

A ₹[X]L-a-month YouTube program: 30 creators a month in Tamil, Hindi, Telugu and English, briefed documentary-style for Tier 2 and 3 audiences.

  • Scouting
  • Negotiation
  • Script reviews
  • AI-query titles
  • Weekly sentiment tracking

The result

60 videos live in two months.

“Fix the source first. Neutral explainers moved AI answers more than glowing reviews did.”
