The Feature · ORM & AEO

Scam allegations to ‘potentially worthwhile’: how 60 videos moved Google’s AI answers.

By Akshaya Reddy · Reputation, search and creators

People researching the courses were getting scam allegations and mixed verdicts straight from Google’s AI Overviews, before they ever reached the website.

The insight

AI answers summarize what the web already says. So instead of arguing with the answer, I changed the sources it pulls from.

What I did

I ran a ₹[X]L-a-month YouTube program with 30 creators a month across Tamil, Hindi, Telugu and English channels, briefed documentary-style for Tier 2 and 3 audiences rather than as testimonials.

I owned scouting and negotiation, reviewed every script, tuned titles and descriptions to the queries that feed AI Overviews, and tracked sentiment every week, adjusting briefs as the answers moved.

The result

60 videos went live in two months, and the AI answers for the brand and its courses turned from doubtful to positive.

“Fix the source first. Neutral explainers moved AI answers more than glowing reviews did.”
