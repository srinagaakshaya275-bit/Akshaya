About

A little about me

I started out curious about why some things get shared and others get scrolled past. That curiosity took me from classrooms to internships to a full-time marketing role — and every step has been about the same thing: making work that people actually stop for.

I like building content, understanding audiences, running campaigns, and testing ideas that might not work. Then I look at the numbers, keep what did work, and rebuild what didn't.

Marketing is part instinct, part spreadsheet. I'm happiest when I get to use both.
