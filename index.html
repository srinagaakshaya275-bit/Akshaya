Featured case study

Rewriting what AI says about a brand

  • ORM
  • AEO
  • Creator Marketing

The problem

People researching Masai's courses were getting scam allegations and mixed verdicts straight from Google's AI Overviews, before they ever reached the website.

The approach

AI answers summarize what the web already says. So instead of arguing with the answer, we changed the sources it pulls from.

What I did

  1. 01Scouted and negotiated with 30 creators a month across Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, and English channels, backed by a ₹8L budget.
  2. 02Briefed creators documentary-style rather than testimonial-style, for Tier 2 and 3 audiences.
  3. 03Reviewed scripts and optimized titles and descriptions to target the queries that feed AI Overviews.
  4. 04Tracked sentiment weekly and reported monthly, adjusting briefs as the answers shifted.

AI Overview sentiment

Before After
  • BITSOM DMScam allegationsPotentially worthwhile
  • BITSOM BACan be goodBest course, job-ready curriculum
  • Roorkee PMDebatedPotentially worthwhile investment
  • MasaiExisting optionGood, intensive, industry-relevant career school

The outcome

What I learned

Fix the source first, because Masai's own fees page was causing part of the pricing confusion.

Neutral, explainer-style videos moved AI answers more than glowing reviews did.

Regional creators reach the audiences that big channels miss.
